- Single candidate calls resolve directly without embedding comparison
- Bare calls (no receiver) are skipped as too ambiguous
- Similarity threshold: 0.7 for embedding resolution
- Semantic relationships: top-10 with min_score 0.5
- `--skip-resolve` skips call resolution but semantic relationships still run
